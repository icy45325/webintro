# Philosophy

> Architecture is not expression. It is a quiet response to site and the ordinary.

## 01 · Site before idea

Every project starts with a long presence. We stay on the site until two things are clear: where the sun comes from, where the wind comes from. Nothing is drawn before that.

## 02 · Material as local grammar

We favour materials found within a fifty-kilometre radius of the site. Not for nostalgia — but because they continue to belong to the land's own cycle when they weather, get repaired, or get torn down.

## 03 · Scale belongs to the user

The spaces we remember are rarely the largest ones. The height of a window, the weight of a door — these are the architecture a person lives through day by day.

## 04 · Handover is not the end

We keep updating project archives for five years after completion. Damage from use, alterations by the owner — all recorded. They are the most honest part of the building.
