## Question

In the language of rural revitalisation, architects tend to show up as "providers of schemes". After we actually stayed in the villages for a few weeks each year, a different picture emerged: the builder is not the architect — it is the returning tiler, the son who trades in town, the relative who drops by twice a year.

## Method

This is a long-cycle archive project. **No drawings. Only questions.** Each year we pick one village, one house, and record three things:

1. Who decided to build this house?
2. Who did each trade?
3. How is the gap between budget, schedule and final dimensions explained — and by whom?

The output is a small booklet, one issue per year, 300 copies, posted back to the people interviewed.
