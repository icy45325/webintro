## Site & Question

The site is a four-hundred-square-metre bamboo grove on the southern slope of Moganshan. Mild grade, high humidity, summer wind from the southeast. The client wanted a two-person residence to repair a life the work had cut too thin. The real constraint was not program but how to keep the architecture from disturbing the grove's existing order.

## Strategy: Step Back

Instead of centring the volume, we pulled it six metres up-slope, yielding a forecourt. The roof pitch is borrowed from the droop of bamboo leaves; charred local timber walls darken in the wet season to match the surrounding bark.

## The Object

The ground floor opens completely to the grove. Two small windows on the upper floor compress the view to a necessary scale — like a pair of binoculars. The first thing the owner sees in the morning is the dew on the leaves.
