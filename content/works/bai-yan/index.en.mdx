## Site & Question

The original building was a 1960s grain store, long disused and reduced to neighbourhood backdrop. Its scale fights the lane, yet its front wall faces the lane's only old scholar tree.

## Strategy: Subtract

We removed the added concrete buttresses and exposed the original brickwork. The roof was relaid with small grey tiles; the interior painted twice with white lime. The only new element is a floor-to-ceiling window opened toward the tree — light filters through the canopy and lands on the shelves.

## The Object

On summer afternoons, cicadas in the scholar tree are audible inside. That is why the old neighbours have started coming back in.
