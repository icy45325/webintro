## Question

The biennial asked for a visitable pavilion built quickly on the square, standing three months. Hard limits: no foundations, no HVAC, no power except for night lighting.

## Strategy

Make it as thin as possible. Eight steel tubes tension an ETFE membrane into four gentle curves — translucent by day, like the underside of a cloud at dusk. Works hang 3.2 m below the membrane; visitors may walk around or through.

## The Object

After three months, the pavilion can be dismantled and transported in eight hours. The columns return to the warehouse; the membrane is cut up as lining for the next project's lampshades.
